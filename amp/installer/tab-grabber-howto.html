Add Tab Grabber to Chrome

Follow these four quick steps to load the unpacked Tab Grabber extension.

  1. 1.Open Chrome's Extensions page
    Copy chrome://extensions and paste it into your browser's address bar (browsers block direct links to internal settings pages).
    chrome://extensions
  2. 2.Turn on Developer mode
    Flip the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.
  3. 3.Click Load unpacked
    Click the Load unpacked button that appears in the top-left toolbar.
